# Privacy Policy for LogMyFit

**Effective date:** November 25, 2026  
**Application:** LogMyFit  
**Developer:** Siwire

LogMyFit is a fitness tracking application for recording exercises, workout sets, training history, and progress. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use the app.

## 1. Information you enter into LogMyFit

You may enter or generate information such as:

- Exercises, categories, and workout templates
- Workout dates, sets, repetitions, weights, distances, and related measurements
- Workout history and progress statistics
- App preferences and settings

This workout information is stored locally on your device. LogMyFit does not require an account, and the developer does not operate an account-based service that automatically receives your workout records.

The app does not intentionally transmit your workout history to the developer or to advertising services.

## 2. Local storage and device backups

LogMyFit uses local device storage to save your workout data and preferences. Data generally remains on the device until you remove it or delete the app's stored data.

Your operating system may provide its own device backup or restore features. Whether app data is included depends on the device, operating-system settings, and the application's backup configuration.

## 3. Importing and exporting workout data

LogMyFit provides optional import and export features for JSON backup files.

When importing a backup, you choose the file through your device's file-selection interface. When exporting a backup, you choose where to save or share it through your device's available options.

LogMyFit does not automatically upload these files to a server operated by the developer. If you choose a third-party service (such as cloud storage, email, or messaging), that service may process the backup under its own privacy policy.

**Exported JSON backup files may contain your workout history and are not necessarily encrypted.** Keep them in a secure location and share them only with parties you trust.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Some versions of LogMyFit may include or enable advertising features provided by **Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)**. Advertising may be unavailable or disabled in certain releases.

Depending on the version, SDK configuration, and applicable consent choices, Google Mobile Ads may collect or process information such as:

- IP address, which may indicate an approximate location
- Advertising and other device or app identifiers
- Interactions with the app and advertisements
- Diagnostic and performance information

Google may use this information for advertising delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. LogMyFit does not intentionally send your workout records to AdMob.

For details, see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Google's advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads), and [Google Mobile Ads data disclosure documentation](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

Where required, the app will provide appropriate advertising privacy and consent choices before applicable data processing. If advertising is enabled, the availability of those choices will depend on your region and applicable law.

## 5. Optional in-app purchases

Some versions of LogMyFit may offer optional in-app purchases processed through **Google Play Billing**.

Google Play processes payments according to its own terms and privacy policy. The developer does not directly receive your full payment-card details from Google Play Billing.

When purchase functionality is enabled, the app may access product identifiers, purchase status, and transaction or purchase-token information as needed to unlock or restore purchased features. Whether this information is transmitted beyond your device depends on the implementation used in that version.

For more information, see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 6. Data sharing

The developer does not sell your workout records. Workout information stored in the app is not automatically shared with third parties by the developer.

Third-party advertising services may process the information described in Section 4 when the relevant SDK functionality is active. Users may also choose to share exported backup files with services or people of their choice.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

You can delete workout information using any available deletion features in LogMyFit, or clear the app's storage through your device settings. Uninstalling the app generally removes its local app data, although device backups or other copies may remain.

Files you previously exported are separate from the app's local storage and must be deleted wherever you saved or shared them.

Data processed by third-party services is subject to those services' retention and deletion policies.

## 8. Security

LogMyFit stores workout records locally rather than automatically uploading them to a developer-operated server. However, no method of storing or transferring data is completely secure.

Protect your device and your exported backup files. Do not assume exported JSON files are encrypted.

## 9. Children's privacy

LogMyFit is intended for general workout tracking and is not specifically designed for children under 13. The developer does not intentionally request children's personal information through a developer-operated account system.

If advertising or purchase features are enabled, their configuration must comply with applicable age-related requirements and platform policies.

## 10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to LogMyFit, third-party integrations, or legal requirements. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
